What runs here?

A small model trained on IBM’s Argument Quality Ranking dataset runs entirely in this page. The first score downloads its weights; your text is never sent for inference.

This edition provides one overall estimate. It does not generate explanations, four rubric subscores, conversation rankings, or factual checks.

The model learns patterns in wording, not a general theory of good reasoning. It can miss context and reward superficial phrasing. Scores are experimental and are not grades or judgments about people.
